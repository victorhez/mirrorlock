# 90-second demo

**0–10s** — “About to make a trade? Let AI challenge it.” Select BTC, BUY, 90% confidence.

**10–25s** — Enter a thesis. Click Challenge my trade. Explain that the product is now testing the user's own reasoning.

**25–45s** — Reveal evidence quality and Confidence Gap. Then show the Mirror case: chasing, volatility, or weak confirmation.

**45–60s** — Show four agents as an understandable decision pipeline, not a wall of agent chat.

**60–72s** — Show the Judge verdict and the “what would change our mind?” section.

**72–82s** — If approved, click Start paper trade. Explain that risk rules remain deterministic.

**82–90s** — Show Paper Trades and Mirror Insights. Close: “The most dangerous AI isn't the one that makes mistakes. It's the one that never questions itself.”
